Research Interests

I’m especially interested in how AI agents learn from experience, retain useful knowledge, and make better decisions over time.

Agent Memory
Episodic and semantic memory, retrieval, and memory consolidation: how agents preserve relevant context, update what they know, and apply past experience to new tasks.
World Models & Decision-Making
JEPA-style predictive representations and action-conditioned world models: how agents can anticipate the consequences of actions and use those predictions for planning.
Local LLMs
Running and adapting open-weight models on local hardware, exploring quantization, efficient inference, and the tradeoffs between model quality, memory use, and latency.
